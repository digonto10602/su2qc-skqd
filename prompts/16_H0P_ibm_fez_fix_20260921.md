## Diagnosis (summary of the memo; the memo has the tables)
The support is sampled from the coarse-step states, not from the ground state.  The missing state (one of the
four B=1 states 40 / 53 / 61 / 65, ground-state weight 9.33e-07 each; state 53 the probable one) has ideal
probability 3.1e-3 to 4.6e-2 in the frozen circuits, so under the frozen shot plan (267 / 130 / 92) its expected
number of clean observations is 0.33, and it was populated in the FakeFez run by a garbage acceptance
(the manual's (1 − f) a term, 0.86 expected hits per B=1 state).  Computed over all sector states, the frozen plan
saturates B=1 from clean shots with probability 0.076 (live calibration) and B=0 with probability 0.004; with
garbage 0.42 and 0.60.  Criterion 3 — correct as written, "Ritz energies of the *saturated* sectors reproduce E_0
to 1e-6", the manual's bit-order check — therefore had a coin-flip outcome in both sectors under the plan of D3,
which was sized for the f comparison only.  The executing agent's inference that the shot budget cannot fix this
is wrong: the expected count scales with the shots, and the manual's shot rule (eq. 5) applied to the rarest
sector state of the frozen circuits gives the required counts.  Nothing in the failure is about the device.
