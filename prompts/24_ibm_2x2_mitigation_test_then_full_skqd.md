Why 6000 and not the pilot's 4000 (planner arithmetic, to be reproduced in the prereg): at $f = 0.0413$, $p_{\rm ref} \approx 0.885$, readout factor 0.82, each circuit gives $6000 \times 0.82 \times 0.0413 \times 0.885 \approx 180$ reference hits, 360 per cell; the ratio of two cells' excess counts has $\sigma_{\ln R} = \sqrt{1/360 + 1/360} = 0.075$, so the 95 % factor is $e^{\pm 0.146} = [0.86, 1.16]$ and a true ratio of 1.25 has lower bound $1.25 \times 0.86 = 1.08 > 1$, i.e. the adoption rule below is decidable at the gain it asks for. - **P6. The decision statistic** is the pooled reference-string clean fraction of each cell (`pooled_reference_string_test` over its two circuits, 68 % and 95 % Garwood intervals), exactly the pilot's S2.  The **ratio** $R_i = f_i / f_0$ equals the ratio of pooled excess counts $X_i / X_0$ (same shots, same $p_{\rm ref}$, same readout factor); its 95 % interval is $\exp(\ln R_i \pm 1.96\sqrt{1/X_i + 1/X_0})$ (Poisson on the excess counts; the garbage expectation, about 1.5 hits per circuit at 6000 shots, is subtracted as in the pilot).  A parametric bootstrap of the same ratio (10 000 draws, seed 11) is reported beside it as a cross-check, never as the verdict.
- **P7. Adoption rule (preregistered, binding).**  A cell $i \in \{T1, T2, T3\}$ *qualifies* iff $R_{i,{\rm lo95}} > 1$ **and** $R_i \ge 1.25$.  Why 1.25: Stage R's shots scale as $1/f$, so a gain $R$ saves $(1 - 1/R)$ of the Stage R execution; at the pilot's 120.6 s, $R = 1.25$ saves 24.1 s, which is the first gain that pays back Stage T's own expected billed cost (about 23 s, table below) — a technique that cannot pay for its test is not worth carrying into the run.  Among qualifying cells the one with the largest point $R_i$ is **adopted** for Stage R; if none qualifies, Stage R runs the baseline configuration (T0).  Family-wise false-positive rate of the rule: three candidates at 2.5 % one-sided each, at most 7.5 % before the 1.25 floor (planner arithmetic; stated in the report).  Separately, the **signed bar** is read on the adopted cell: GO iff $f_{{\rm adopted},{\rm lo95}} \ge 0.1$, NO-GO iff $f_{{\rm adopted},{\rm hi95}} < 0.1$, AMBIGUOUS otherwise — information for the owner; Stage R proceeds in every case under the owner's decision.  Planner expectation, in writing: the signed bar will not be reached (it needs $R \gtrsim 2.4$); whether any cell qualifies is genuinely open — the Aer null is $R = 0.82$–$0.94$ and the ceiling is $\times 4.3$ (report section 4).

## Honest limits (to be stated verbatim, with the JSON's numbers)
- Stage T compares four cells inside one job on one calibration content; its ratios carry no statement about other days or patches.  The null ratios ($e^{-S_{\rm DD}}$) assume the record's $x$ error for every inserted pulse.
- Three candidates are tested; the family-wise chance that a null cell qualifies on the interval alone is ≤ 7.5 %, and the 1.25 floor is a cost argument, not a physics threshold.
- A qualifying ratio raises $f$ for *this* circuit's idle structure; the signed bar is read on the adopted cell's lower 95 % bound and nothing else.
- Stage R runs below the signed budget by the owner's decision; the sectors saturate from accidentally-valid noise at these shot counts (`C22_saturation`), so $E_R(B_{\rm all})$ reproducing $E_0$ is a property of the decoder (gate E2), not of the device; $B_{\rm sig}$ excludes uniform noise but not near-clean false positives (M4.4); the garbage and random baselines are the controls that make the number readable.
- Two $k=1$ circuits (Stage T) and seven (Stage R) are not a family-level statement at other $k$; the $k=4$ cells' $f$ is information (C6 ruling).
- One calibration content per stage; drift between submission and retrieval is reported, not corrected.

## Escalation and STOP conditions
- Kingston unreachable / not operational / missing error leaves on the patch: STOP, record, hand back.
- Remaining seconds < 275 at T.A1, Stage T estimate > 30 s, or `40 + reserve > remaining`: STOP with the numbers; no resizing.
- After Stage T: the recomputed Stage R reserve does not fit the remaining seconds (threshold about $f_{\rm pool} < 0.018$): **STOP to the owner**; no resizing without the owner.
- Fingerprint moved at a calwatch or refused by the preflight: D10 (re-run the record/prereg steps on the new content, no parameter change), at most twice per stage, then STOP.
- Job ERROR / CANCELLED: record; no automatic resubmission; the owner decides whether the retry margin is spent.
- A dry run fails on a K/R criterion: fix the analysis, re-run; never edit a counts file; two honest failures → hand back with the traceback.
- Stage T AMBIGUOUS on the signed bar: information only; Stage R proceeds (owner's decision) with the adopted cell.
- R5 consistency FAIL (Stage R's $f$ outside 30 % of Stage T's): the gate records FAIL; the physics blocks are still computed and reported; hand back — no re-run.
- Any analysis number disagreeing with its pilot counterpart beyond the stated tolerance (the plan arithmetic test, the garbage acceptances): stop and report before submitting.
- If the executor believes a criterion or tolerance above is wrong: STOP and say so; do not loosen it.
