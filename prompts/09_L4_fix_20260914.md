  noisy sampling costs 1.72 s per shot (50 shots in 86 s; 10 shots in 26 s); noiseless 1000 shots take 17 s.

## Diagnosis
`scripts/laptop_L4_aer_noise.py` measures a fixed pilot of 1000 noisy shots per sector before choosing the
shots per circuit, and clamps the choice to `max(500, …)`.  At 1.72 s per shot the pilot alone is 1 720 s per
sector and the 500-shot floor over 28 circuits is another 24 000 s: the `--budget-minutes` argument cannot
be honoured, so the run can never finish on this laptop with the generic-synthesis circuits of gate L3
(35 606 CZ per coarse step, `validation/L3.json`).  This is the S2 depth problem (prompt 03/04 escalation
clause), not a physics or noise-model error: with f = (1 − 3e-3)^35670 ≈ 3e-47 the proxy yield is zero and
the gate's yield criterion is expected to FAIL; the deliverable of this prompt is the JSON/report documenting
the reduced run and the measured per-shot rate.

## Fix
`scripts/laptop_L4_aer_noise.py`, `main()`: add two arguments with the current behaviour as default —
`--pilot-shots` (int, default 1000) used for the pilot instead of the literal 1000, and `--min-shots`
(int, default 500) used as the floor instead of the literal 500 in `shots = int(min(20000, max(500, …)))`.
Divide the pilot time by `args.pilot_shots`.  Print the pilot rate and the chosen shots per sector to stdout
as soon as they are known (one line each), so that a killed run still leaves a trace in the log.  Record
`pilot_shots` and `min_shots` in `R.data` for each sector and print them in the report header line next to
the budget.  Change nothing else: no criterion, no threshold, no noise model, no circuits.
