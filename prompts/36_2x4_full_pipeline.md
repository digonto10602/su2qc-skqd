## 1. Rulings

**R1 — what is sampled: the IR-L0 family, on the Aer GPU, from the committed QPY bundles.**  The noiseless
pipeline test samples the signed family itself (the structured IR of `circuit_set(4)` as a qiskit circuit through
`circuits_qiskit.ir_to_qiskit`, the "IR-L0" family of campaign 33, `src/skqd/campaign33/circuits.py`
`BASE_FAMILIES`), not the Quantinuum-native conversions: at 2x4 the native circuits cost 3.4x (opt3) to 6.0x
(structured) more GPU time per coarse step (480 509 / 847 670 against 142 288 instructions, `ci/parts/QV_2x4_01.json`)
for the same physics, and C1_IDEAL at 2x3 sampled IR-L0 for the plan runs.  The native family's own
measurement (Q1 of Q0P_2x4) continues in slots 06-07 on the k = 1 circuits of the source slot 01 freezes (R7).
Every circuit the GPU runs is loaded from a committed `qpy13` gzip bundle whose gz sha256 is in the slot's part
file (the S2_2x4 lesson: the CI never rebuilds a circuit; `data/S2_2x4/qpy13_3.json`).

**R2 — the decoder's random acceptance at 2x4 is computed exactly, not assumed.**  An exhaustive 2^28 loop
through `Codec.decode` costs 256 x the 34.5 s of 2x3 (*planner arithmetic*: 2.5 h in Python), so the count is
done by the structure of the decoder: `Codec.decode` accepts a string iff every vertex pattern decodes without
`flag`, the two ends of every link agree (`link`), the baryon number is the target (`sector`) and the label is in
`basis.index` (`unknown`).  Implement `scripts/gate_H0P.py: random_acceptance_factorised(codec, twoB)`: enumerate
per vertex the 2^w patterns and keep those `decode_vertex` accepts (with their flux / n / iota), join vertices
over the links by a dictionary keyed on the link values (the join is small: it enumerates exactly the
link-consistent products), apply the sector test, then the `basis.index` lookup; return the same dictionary as
`random_acceptance` (`accepted`, `strings`, `fraction`, `reasons` for `sector` and `unknown`; `flag` and `link`
counts are derived from the per-vertex and per-link rejections and must reproduce the exhaustive 2x3 numbers).
Criterion A1: the factorised count equals the exhaustive one at 2x2 (`validation/H0P*.json` record, or the
exhaustive loop re-run at 2^12 in seconds) and at 2x3 (677 / 426 and the four reason counts of
`data/cv/acceptance_2x3.json`, exactly); at 2x4 the count is written to `data/cv/acceptance_2x4.json` together
with a Monte-Carlo cross-check (1e7 uniformly random 28-bit strings through `Codec.decode`, seed 36, about
6 min at the 2x3 rate; the accepted count must lie within 3 sigma of 1e7 x fraction).  *Planner prediction, to be
falsified*: accepted = 12843 / 8934 (the decoder is injective onto the codewords, as at 2x3), i.e. fractions
4.785e-5 / 3.328e-5 (*planner arithmetic*, dim / 2^28).  If the count differs from the dimension, `Phys.sector[tb]["a"]`
is corrected to read the file and the finding is recorded (it changes the B_sig cut of every 2x4 reading).
