## Goal

Measure, with 0 QPU s, whether the one encoding the planner ranked as worth testing (**E2 "dedup"**: one flux bit
per link instead of one per link end, one bit per vertex) and the one free optimisation inside the current encoding
(**E5' codeword-assignment search**) shorten the signed 2x3 coarse-step circuits -- in qubits, two-qubit gates and
depth -- at *fixed physics*, and what each costs in the Gauss-law error detection the decoder provides.  Produce one
comparison table (gate `ENC_compare`) that the owner can read next to `validation/S2.json`.

What this is NOT: no new physics target (the Hamiltonian, basis, truncation and $\Delta t$ are the signed ones);
no replacement of the signed circuit family (the dedup circuits are *candidates*, flagged `unsigned`); no device
run; no change to the plan of record.

**Definition of "shortens" (fixed by this prompt, judged per lattice at 2x2 and 2x3):** at the same $\Delta t$
(`data/references.json`), the same 44-circuit signed family at 2x3 (28 at 2x2: references x $k=1..4$ from
`gate_S2D.circuit_set`), the same term order (`krylov.term_groups`), the same transpiler settings as gate S2
(basis `rz, sx, x, cz`, optimization level 3, seeds 7/8/9 all-to-all, seed 7 routed on `CouplingMap.from_heavy_hex`
3 / 5) and the same Quantinuum native path as campaign33's chosen variant (`compile_native(..., optimisation_level=2)`,
NAT-O2), and under the S2 exactness bars (circuit vs `coarse_states` $\le10^{-10}$, leakage $\le10^{-9}$), a
candidate **shortens** if (i) `n_qubits` is smaller, (ii) the mean all-to-all CZ count of the coarse step over the
three seeds is smaller by more than the three-seed spread (max-min) of the current encoding, and (iii) the routed CZ
count and the native ZZ count are both smaller.  It **shortens meaningfully** if the two-qubit ratios are $\le0.5$
(the planner's threshold for trading away the decoder; owner's call, section 6).  Depth is reported, not judged.

2. **Stage `equiv` (10 min).** For 2x2 and 2x3, both codecs: encode-decode round trip on every state; codewords
   distinct; exhaustive random acceptance per sector (all $2^{N_q}$ strings for $N_q\le13$; for the current 2x3 codec
   the exact value $\dim/2^{20}$, stated as exact because the decoder accepts only valid labels -- verify on $10^6$
   uniformly random strings with seed 7 and report the count); per-term exactness of every dedup term against
   $e^{-i\theta H_\gamma}$ on 20 random codeword superpositions (seed 7) at $\theta=\Delta t_{B=0}$ and
   $4\Delta t_{B=0}$; the 28 (2x2) and 44 (2x3) dedup coarse circuits against `krylov.coarse_states` (deviation and
   leakage, S2 bars $10^{-10}$ / $10^{-9}$) through `CodewordEmbedding(codec=DedupCodec)` and `run_ir`.  Spectra
   and $E_0$: the encoding does not change $H$; record `data.equiv.hamiltonian_unchanged = true` with the sha256 of
   the term matrices, and the per-sector $E_0$ read from `data/references.json` (no recomputation).

3. **Stage `compile` (15 min).** For 2x2 and 2x3, both codecs, every term and every coarse circuit of the signed
   family: IR counts (`gate_counts`), `transpile_counts` all-to-all at seeds 7, 8, 9 (CZ, 1q = sx + x, rz, depth,

4. **Stage `native` (15 min).** The 44 2x3 circuits of both codecs through `ir_to_pytket` -> `compile_native(H2-2,

   report must say so).

5. **Stage `kingston` (2 x about 20-25 min, background, one invocation per circuit).** `B0_ref117_k1` (mandatory)
   and `B1_ref29_k1` (optional if the first stayed under 30 min) in both codecs through the K0 path: exactness-gated
   routing (`route`, same seed rule), ALAP schedule with explicit delays (`alap`), on the record
   `data/hardware/K0_prep/ibm_kingston_full_20261006T0652Z.json`; record CZ, active qubits, `scheduled_duration_s`,
   `f_gates_layout`, `f_best_patch_bound`, and `idle_terms` at echo $T_2$ and at $0.174\,T_2$ (both, as K0).  The
   current-codec numbers must reproduce `validation/K0_2x3_2x4.json` for that circuit (5 659 CZ, 411.2 us) within
   the recorded tolerance of K0.4; if the dedup routing is not exact under the K0 bar after the K0 seed set, record
   `kingston.dedup.not_exact = true` and stop this stage (do not loosen the bar).

6. **Stage `f` (5 min).** Gate-only $f$ with `device_req.clean_shot_fraction` on the native counts with the H2-2
   triple exactly as `quantinuum_device_table.row` does (same eps_ro convention), per circuit and as mean / worst
   over the family (the amendment-01 bars 0.1 / 0.05 are *reported*, not applied); the H2-2 memory ESTIMATE
   scenarios (low / mid / high) through the same function as the survey; kingston gate-only and idle-aware $f$ from
   stage 5.  Also the projected 2x4 numbers by formula only (qubits 28 vs 18) unless `--2x4` is given (optional:
   structured compile of the dedup 2x4 terms with a 30-minute cap and no routing; the current 2x4 counts are read
   from `validation/S2_2x4.json`).

7. **Stage `detect` (5 min).** Exhaustive single-flip detection per sector for both codecs at 2x2 and 2x3 (fraction
   detected; by reason; silently accepted as another valid state), double flips at 2x2; random acceptance $a$;
   the saturation parameter $N a/\dim$ at the plan-of-record shots (34 909 / 99 000) and the $B_{\rm sig}$ $3\sigma$
   line $\mu_s+3\sqrt{\mu_s}$ with $\mu_s=Na/\dim$, against $\lambda^*=6.2958$.  The prototype values to reproduce:
   current 2x3 98.17 / 98.31 %, dedup 83.05 / 90.75 %; $a$ = 677/8192 and 426/8192; saturation 4.26 / 12.1.

8. **Stage `assign` (E5', about 20 min; `--quick` subsamples).** Within the current codec widths and with the flux
   bits fixed (they carry the link check), enumerate the free codeword assignments: interior vertex -- which of the
   two 4-bit patterns of each even-parity flux pattern is $n=0$ vs $n=2$ ($2^4$), which pattern is the flag for each
   one-flux pattern ($2^3$), the $\iota$ bit polarity for the three-flux pattern (2): 256 assignments, the same at
   both interior vertices; corner -- $n/2$ polarity for equal flux (2 x 2) and the flag choice for unequal flux (2):
   8 assignments, the same at all corners.  Score = all-to-all CZ (seed 7) of the nine 2x3 terms compiled
   structurally; report the best interior assignment on {hop4, plaq1} and the best corner assignment on
   {hop0, hop1, plaq0}, the resulting coarse-step CZ, and the detection fractions of the best assignment (they must
   be identical to the current ones: same flags, same link check).  This stage changes **no** production code: the
   assignment is a parameter of a `Codec` subclass in the script (`codec_variant.py` may live under `scripts/`).
   Record `assign.best_step_cz_ratio`.

9. **Stage `assemble`.** Criteria C1-C8, status, the comparison table, `reports/ENC_compare.md`, `validation/gates.md`
   row via `update_status.py`, `prompts/LOG.md` row, commit.
