## 1. What was asked

Find the current public numbers for Quantinuum H2-2 (and H2-1) and Helios-1: gate and SPAM errors, memory/idle and transport errors, T1/T2, gate durations and parallelism, qubit counts, the HQC cost formula and the emulator's noise model.  Record each value with its URL, access date, source date and qualification.

## 2. Actions taken

I downloaded the H2 and Helios product data sheets (PDF), the Performance Validation page (machine-specific randomized-benchmarking numbers), the emulator noise-model pages, the operation pages and the costing page from docs.quantinuum.com, and the Helios (arXiv:2511.05465) and H2 (arXiv:2305.03828) papers from arXiv. Randomized benchmarking (RB) means: run random sequences of gates of growing length and fit the decay of the success probability; the decay rate per gate is the *average gate infidelity* (one minus the average fidelity).  SPAM means state preparation and measurement error.

### 3.3 Durations, parallelism, gate set, cost

- **H2-2, native_gates:** ['Rz (virtual)', 'PhasedX / Rxy(theta, phi)', 'ZZ = exp(-i pi/4 ZZ)', 'RZZ(theta) = exp(-i theta/2 ZZ)', 'general SU(4) entangler'] ([ds_h2](https://docs.quantinuum.com/systems/_static/assets/data_sheets/Quantinuum%20H2%20Product%20Data%20Sheet.pdf)).
- **Helios-1, native_gates:** ['Rz (virtual)', 'Rxy(theta, phi)', 'ZZ = exp(-i pi/4 ZZ)', 'RZZ(theta) = exp(-i theta/2 ZZ)'] — minimum RZZ angle 1e-4, minimum Rxy angle 3e-4 (smaller angles rounded to zero; helios_ops) ([ds_helios](https://docs.quantinuum.com/systems/_static/assets/data_sheets/Quantinuum%20Helios%20Product%20Data%20Sheet.pdf)).
- **H2-2, gate_durations:** not published — not published per gate; H2 paper Table 1 gives shot time 0.72 s for a 32-qubit RCS circuit with 172 2Q gates in 56 2Q gate rounds (17 ms/shot fixed overhead) ([h2_paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03828)).
- **Helios-1, gate_durations:** not published — per-gate durations not published; ground-state cooling ~3 ms (paper App.) ([helios_paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05465)).
- **Helios-1, parallel_2q_operations:** 4 — 8 gate zones for SPAM and 1q operations, 4 zones for 2q operations; 'This system requires 7 gating rounds to perform 2-qubit gates on all [N/2] pairs for a maximally dense circuit' (helios_ops) ([ds_helios](https://docs.quantinuum.com/systems/_static/assets/data_sheets/Quantinuum%20Helios%20Product%20Data%20Sheet.pdf)).
- **H2-2, rzz_angle_dependence_paper:** epsilon(theta) = (2.9(2) theta/pi + 0.46(6)) x 1e-3 — H2 at launch (2023), zone-averaged direct RB of U_ZZ(theta); not the current machine ([h2_paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03828)).
- **H2-2, hqc_formula:** HQC = 5 + C/5000 (N_1q + 10 N_2q + 5 N_m), C = shots, N_1q = PhasedX count (Rz excluded), N_2q = ZZ/RZZ count, N_m = measurements+resets+initialisations — published on the Helios costing page as the HQC formula (Max(...) over shots for dynamic programs) ([costing](https://docs.quantinuum.com/systems/trainings/helios/getting_started/costing.html)).
- **Helios-1, hqc_formula:** same as H2 (HQC = 5 + C/5000 (N_1q + 10 N_2q + 5 N_m)); Helios jobs limited to 10 000 shots and 500 000 HQC ([costing](https://docs.quantinuum.com/systems/trainings/helios/getting_started/costing.html)).

The HQC formula in LaTeX: $$\mathrm{HQC}=5+\frac{C}{5000}\left(N_{1q}+10\,N_{2q}+5\,N_{m}\right),$$ with $C$ the number of shots, $N_{1q}$ the PhasedX count, $N_{2q}$ the ZZ/RZZ count and $N_m$ the number of measurements, resets and initialisations.

### 3.4 The emulator's noise model (vendor description)

- p1 / p2: probability of a fault in a 1q / 2q gate, modelled mostly as asymmetric depolarizing channels; a fraction (p1_emission_ratio, p2_emission_ratio) is spontaneous emission instead: X 1/4, Y 1/4, leakage 1/2 of the time.
- p_meas: classical bit-flip on measurement, separately for 0 and 1 outcomes; p_init: initialisation fault; crosstalk faults on MCMR.
- RZZ(theta) depolarizing strength scales with angle: (przz_a |theta/pi|^przz_power + przz_b) p2, normalised so theta = pi/2 gives p2.
- Memory (dephasing): Z noise during idling and transport, quadratic (coherent RZ(f d) in statevector mode, incoherent Pauli Z with probability sin^2(f d / 2) in stabilizer mode) and linear (Pauli Z with probability r d), d = duration.
- Helios emulators add leakage/seepage; H2 emulators do not.

## 4. What the result means

- The machine-specific H2-2 numbers used by the package and by reviewer-opus (2q 0.00083, 1q 2.8e-05, SPAM 0.00067 / 0.0012) are the ones currently on the public Performance Validation page; the reviewer's $f$ for H2-2 therefore rests on public data.
- Helios-1 has a lower 2q error than H2-2 but a larger published memory error per depth-1 layer (linear plus quadratic parts), measured with 98 qubits; how much of that a 20- or 28-qubit circuit pays is not published.
- Neither system publishes T1, T2 or per-gate durations; idling is published as a memory error per depth-1 layer instead (a depth-1 layer = re-pair all qubits and apply 1q and 2q gates to all of them).  The noise model in report 002 therefore charges memory per depth-1-equivalent layer, not per second.

## 5. Problems and assumptions

- The Performance Validation page gives no date for its RB sweep (copyright 2025); the Helios data sheet is dated 2026-06-09 and the H2 sheet 2025-09-17.
- The H2 emulator page heads the H2-2E table 'H2-1E emulators from 2025-07-16' (a vendor typo or copy); recorded as found.
- The RZZ angle dependence is published only for the emulator (and in the 2023 H2 paper for the machine at launch); the model uses the emulator's form.
- Helios standard SPAM reads leaked ions as 1, so its |1> SPAM under-counts; the ternary measurement has a larger error.
