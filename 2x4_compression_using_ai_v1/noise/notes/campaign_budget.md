2. **Noisy plan**: $N_c' = \lceil N_c / f_c \rceil_{100}$ with $f_c = f_\mathrm{gate}(c)\,(1-\bar\epsilon_\mathrm{ro})^n$, the model's clean-shot fraction of circuit $c$ on the device (a clean shot is one with no fault anywhere, readout included; $\bar\epsilon_\mathrm{ro}$ is the mean readout flip probability, $n$ the qubit count); $N_\mathrm{eq}' = \lceil N_\mathrm{eq} / \bar f \rceil_{100}$ with $\bar f$ the sector mean of $f_c$.  $\lceil x \rceil_{100}$ = $x$ rounded up to a multiple of 100.  This keeps the expected number of *clean* shots per circuit equal to the noiseless plan, which is what D3'-R sizes.
3. **Sampled shots**: $\max(N_c', N_\mathrm{eq}')$ for $k \le 4$ and $N_\mathrm{eq}'$ for $k = 5$; the plan point is the first $N_c'$ shots of each sequence, CV1/CV2/CV4 read the prefixes $\varphi N_c'$, $\varphi = 1/16, \dots, 1$, CV3 the first $N_\mathrm{eq}'$.
4. **Cost** = sampled shots x (mean measured s / trajectory x 1.15) + 10 s per chunk; chunks of at most 900 s (half of the 30-minute cap).
5. **Wall time at 10 threads** = thread-hours / (10 x 0.68).  The factor 0.68 is an **assumption, not a measurement**: the laptop has 6 physical cores with 2 hardware threads each (CPUs k and k+6 share a core), so 10 busy threads put two on 4 of the 6 cores; I assumed each of those runs at 0.6 of a lone thread, $(4\cdot 2\cdot 0.6 + 2)/10 = 0.68$.  At 5 threads (CPUs 5-9) the probes measured the rate directly.

What the cuts mean:

- **2x4 B=1 at the 1x D3'-R plan** (the plan before the CV re-size): the D3'-R guarantee is kept (with clean shots alone, recall of $S_{99.9}$ at least 0.9 with probability at least 0.95, `validation/CV_2x4_plan.json` `P_recall_S999_ge_target`), but in the noiseless emulation CV1/CV2 at B=1 needed the x12 re-size, so CV1/CV2 at 2x4 B=1 are expected to fail at this size for reasons of shots, not of noise; the ideal samples of the same size are the control for that.
- **Helios 2x4 classB_v2 at half the f-scaled shots**: the clean-count guarantee holds at half strength ($\lambda^*/2$ expected clean counts per $S_{99}$ state).
- **Skipped cells** keep the existing short noise runs (`noise_behaviour`) and the analytic clean fraction (`analytic_f`) of reports/003.
