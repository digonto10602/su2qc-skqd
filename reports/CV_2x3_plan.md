## Notes

- **three_seeds**: three seeds cannot establish the 95 % probability of the D3'-R rule; the conservative reading (every one of the three seeds must pass) is used
- **width_no_plateau**: no plateau constant is imposed on the certificate width: the Weinstein certificate is rigorous for any B, it must meet the H1/H2 target, and shrinking further with shots is not a requirement of H1/H2
- **B_sig_nesting**: the prefixes nest B_all and the counts exactly; B_sig is NOT nested by construction, because its threshold mu_s = N a / dim rises with N (a state with the same count at a larger N can fall below the one-sided 3 sigma level).  CV0 therefore checks nesting and monotonicity on B_all and on the counts, and reports the number of B_sig non-nested steps as information
- **gap_B1**: at B=1 gap_assumption_holds is expected false (near-degenerate cluster, splitting 0.024, manual 1.5); the rigorous statement is 'some eigenvalue in [E_R - r_H, E_R + r_H]' (manual 5.3)
- **random_baseline_solver**: random baselines: lowest eigenpair of the sparse projected block by Lanczos (scipy eigsh, tol 1e-12), agreeing with the dense eigh to ~3e-14; device bases: skqd.skqd.ritz
- **cv4_points**: CV4 is evaluated at every shots-curve point phi = 1/16..1 and every equal-shots k = 1..4 (the criterion curves), plus the N/2 and N points of a re-sized plan; the allocation and 2 N_c curves are information
- **cause_of_failure**: CV2 and CV3 fail on the WIDTH condition (r_H <= 0.1 at B=0, 0.15 at B=1) on B_sig; CV1, CV4, CV5 and CV0 hold everywhere.  The ladder table shows why re-sizing cannot rescue B=0: B_sig keeps a state only when its count is one-sided 3 sigma above mu_s = N a / dim, and mu_s grows linearly with N, so the count a state needs to enter B_sig rises with every doubling (2 counts at the 1x plan, 4-5 at 8x), and the low-weight neighbours that set r_H stay out; on B_all the same samples meet the width at 8x (column 'max r_H(sN/2) B_all').  CV3 fails on the width at every cell and, at B=1, also on the energy plateau in several seeds; on the 2 N_c curve the k plateau holds in most seeds while the width does not (table 'CV3 on the 2 N_c curve').  The re-run of the chunks on the plan read from validation/Q0P_2x3_plan.json (same samples, cache) reproduced every value: a second failure under the retry rule, so the gate is BLOCKED and the planner returns.  No constant, tolerance, the P9 rule or the signed family was changed
- **resize_floor**: re-sized floor circuits get ceil100(267 s) shots; at s = 8 that is 2,200 > the 8 x 267 = 2,136 sampled, so those circuits are evaluated at 2,136 (fewer shots: conservative) and listed
