## 3. Re-scheduling headroom inside the exact family

The frozen canary runs 663 CZ in 455 CZ layers
(1.457 CZ per layer), i.e. it is serial in its two-qubit gates.  Qubit-time
utilisation (busy / (qubits x T)) is **0.233**.

For *any* reordering of the same gates on the same layout, no qubit can finish before its own busy
time, so `T_min = max_q busy_q` bounds the duration from below, and the idle budget evaluated with one
window per qubit (the window structure that minimises the PTA budget at fixed total idle) bounds
`S_T1 + S_T2` from below:

Why the schedule is serial: the coarse step is a product of 6 term
exponentials whose qubit supports are listed below.  Only
**1 of the 15** pairs of terms have disjoint
support; the only disjoint pair is hop1 / hop3.  The diagonal term touches all
12 logical qubits and the plaquette

parallel with anything else: the overlap that serialises the circuit is a property of the
Hamiltonian's supports under this codeword layout, not of the synthesis.
